HOW TO READ A ROW
  Tower = Embedding -> conv filters(per layer) -> global pool. The two tower
  outputs are concatenated and fed to Head MLP (concat_dim -> hidden -> ... -> 1).
  Default pool = global-max; '+mean' = max & mean concat (2x width);
  'proj32' = each tower squeezed to 32 dims before the head.

  balAcc = BALANCED ACCURACY = (sensitivity + specificity)/2, after binarizing
  BOTH truth and prediction into active/inactive at a threshold. Robust to the
  heavy class imbalance (few actives). The 'balAcc' column below is the MEAN
  over the dataset's threshold panel; the per-threshold breakdown is in the
  BALANCED ACCURACY section at the bottom. Thresholds (from ../test.py):
    davis/bindingdb: 5.0 5.5 6.0 6.5 7.0 7.5 8.0 8.5
    kiba:            10.0 10.5 11.0 11.5 12.0 12.1 12.5

BOTTOM LINE
==========================================================================================
1. On balanced accuracy the story shifts vs MSE: specificity is ~0.98 for all
   models (class imbalance), so balAcc is driven by SENSITIVITY (catching rare
   actives). Models with worse MSE can rank higher on balAcc (e.g. davis c5inv).
2. Full CNN baseline is still top or near-top on balAcc on every dataset.
3. TARGET (Davis): match DeepDTAGen's per-threshold BA within 2pp. On the 7
   defined thresholds (5.5-8.5) DeepDTAGen means 0.797; our best CNNs
   (t1 0.807, baseline 0.783) are already in range there. Threshold 5.0 is
   undefined for our '>=' scoring (Davis min = 5.0 -> no negatives).
4. balAcc for DeepDTA-proper and DeepDTAGen on KIBA/BindingDB is still unknown
   (not in the paper). DeepDTAGen weights are present and could be re-scored.
5. REGULARIZATION SWEEP (Davis, balAcc-selected checkpoints): the c3-dilated
   800K model at dropout 0.1 / wd 0 (regB_d1_wd0) reaches balMean 0.848 and
   MEETS OR BEATS the DeepDTAGen target at EVERY threshold (8.0: .82 vs .75,
   8.5: .84 vs .71). Adding dropout or weight-decay MONOTONICALLY LOWERED balAcc
   in both configs -> overfitting (an MSE story) is NOT what caps balAcc; the
   lever was selecting the checkpoint by balAcc. The MPC-ideal 267K model
   (regA_d1_wd0) lands balMean 0.795, level with DeepDTAGen and within 2pp
   everywhere (beats it at 8.5: .74 vs .71).
