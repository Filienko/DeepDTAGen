HOW TO READ A ROW
  Tower = Embedding -> conv filters(per layer) -> global pool. The two tower
  outputs are concatenated and fed to Head MLP (concat_dim -> hidden -> ... -> 1).
  Default pool = global-max; '+mean' = max & mean concat (2x width);
  'proj32' = each tower squeezed to 32 dims before the head.

  balAcc = BALANCED ACCURACY = (sensitivity + specificity)/2, after binarizing
  BOTH truth and prediction into active/inactive at a threshold. Robust to the
  heavy class imbalance (few actives). The 'balAcc' column below is the MEAN
  over the dataset's threshold panel; the per-threshold breakdown is in the
  BALANCED ACCURACY section at the bottom. Thresholds (from ../test.py):
    davis/bindingdb: 5.0 5.5 6.0 6.5 7.0 7.5 8.0 8.5
    kiba:            10.0 10.5 11.0 11.5 12.0 12.1 12.5

   800K model at dropout 0.1 / wd 0 (regB_d1_wd0) reaches balMean 0.848 and

6. BATCH 2026-07-04 (17 runs, all balAcc-selected). Key conclusions:
   a. A1 ABLATION resolves the confound: the missing 2x2 cell (no-dilation +
      balAcc-sel) = 0.831, vs no-dil+MSE-sel 0.787, dil+MSE-sel 0.767,
      dil+balAcc-sel 0.848. => balAcc-CHECKPOINT-SELECTION is the real lever
      (+0.04 to +0.08); DILATION adds only ~+0.017 on top (and HURT under
      MSE-sel). The 0.848 winner was mostly selection, not dilation.
   b. WINNER across seeds is noisy: 0.848(4221)/0.804(s7)/0.823(s13) => the
      true value is ~0.82 +/- 0.02, not a reliable 0.848. Still within target.
   c. POOLING: same stable config, MAX=0.841 vs MEAN=0.781 (-6pp). Mean pool
      is cheaper in MPC but costs real balAcc on Davis -- a genuine tradeoff.

7. TOWER-ABLATION + PROTEIN-TOWER INTERPRETABILITY (2026-07-06). Full writeup:
   EXPERIMENTS.md section 9. Answers: how important is each modality alone, and
   can the learned protein CNN be replaced by a hand-coded rule?
   a. MODALITY ABLATION (retrain a single tower from scratch, not just zero an
      input): drug-tower-only (no protein at all) = balAcc 0.586, MSE ~0.69 --
      clears the mean-affinity floor (0.500/0.80) by a lot but nowhere near the
      full model (0.841/0.267). protein-tower-only (no drug at all) = balAcc
      0.539, MSE ~0.75 -- barely above the floor. On Davis, DRUG IDENTITY alone
      carries far more signal than PROTEIN IDENTITY alone.
   b. INTERPRETABILITY: single-channel knockout on the protein tower's final
      (96-channel) conv layer, then motif extraction via argmax-position under
      max-pool + the exact receptive-field formula RF=(kernel-1)*sum(dilations)+1.
      The top-ranked channels on TWO different checkpoints (plain 882K and
      [1,2,4]-dilated 800K) independently rediscovered real, textbook kinase
      catalytic motifs (HRD box, DFG box, APE motif) with zero biological priors.
   c. TWO BUGS CAUGHT before trusting the interpretability numbers -- both real,
      both worth remembering: (i) scoring the rare/ambiguous residue code 'X'
      against its true background frequency (~5e-6) let log-odds blow up from
      pseudocounts alone -- fixed by restricting PWM scoring to the 20 standard
      amino acids. (ii) 4 of 5 fitted PWMs were contaminated by an N-terminal/
      tag-region artifact: 53-84% of test proteins' 'best-matching window' for
      those channels sat at literal sequence position 0 (every protein starts
      with Met + a similar tag-like region) -- fixed via --min-window-pos to
      drop boundary-adjacent hits. Side effect: channel 74, the single highest-
      knockout-importance channel, only has 47 genuine non-artifact hits (vs 246
      for channel 84) -- its importance may be partly a shortcut, not biology.
   d. RULE-BASED REPLACEMENT (the payoff): swap the ENTIRE learned protein CNN
      for just 5 fixed PWM motif-match scores (drug tower still learned). Result
      (avg 2 seeds): balAcc 0.733 (MSE 0.38) with the artifact FIXED, vs 0.723
      contaminated, vs 0.841 for the full 96-channel learned tower, vs 0.586
      drug-only. => 5 hand-scannable motifs recover ~58% of the drug-only-to-
      full-model gap, at 687K params (vs 882K) with NO learned protein-side
      compute at all -- just a sliding-window scan. Cleaned-PWM version beats
      the contaminated one, confirming the N-terminal artifact was net noise.
   e. STILL OPEN: whether the 96 protein-tower channels are mostly redundant
      with each other or genuinely mostly unused (deferred by request); whether
      the drug/protein balance shifts on KIBA (4x more data, less protein reuse).
